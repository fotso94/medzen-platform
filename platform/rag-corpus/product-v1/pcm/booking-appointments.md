# How To Book Appointment

## How I go fit book appointment for MedZen?
Open the MedZen app, go to Appointments, then click Book New Appointment. Pick the type of appointment wey you want, like in-person, video consultation or follow-up, choose a healthcare provider or make MedZen match you with one, pick a date and time wey dey available, then confirm. You go receive confirmation and reminder before the day of the visit.

## Wetin kind appointment I fit book?
You fit book general consultation, video consultation, follow-up visit, specialist consultation, lab work like blood draw, or emergency walk-in visit. Video consultations dey usually shorter pass in-person visits, and specialist consultations dey usually take longer time. The exact time e go take dem go show you as you dey book.

## How I go reschedule appointment?
Go to My Appointments (My Appointments) for MedZen app, select the appointment and click Reschedule, then pick new time. You fit reschedule up to small time before the appointment reach, and the app go tell you the last time you fit do am for your provider.

## How I go cancel appointment, and dem go charge me?
Go to My Appointments, select the appointment and click Cancel. If you cancel early, normally at least 24 hours before the appointment, e no go cost you anything. If you cancel late or you miss appointment, the provider fit charge you based on the policy wey dem show you when you dey book.

## Wetin go happen if I miss my appointment?
If you miss appointment without cancel am, the provider fit charge you no-show fee based on the policy wey dem show you when you book. You fit book new appointment anytime from the Appointments screen.

## When providers dey available?
Availability dey depend on each provider and care center, and na only free slots dem go show you when you dey book. Some providers dey available for evening or weekend. If na urgent matter outside normal hours, use the Emergency (Emergency) button for the app or contact your local emergency services.
