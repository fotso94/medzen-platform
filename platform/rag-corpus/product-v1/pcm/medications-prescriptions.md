# Medications and Prescriptions (Medicine and Doctor Paper)

## Where I fit see my prescriptions?
Open MedZen app, go Health Records, then Medications. Every prescription go show the medicine name, how much you go take, how often you go take am, the doctor wey write am and how the refill dey go.

## How I fit request medicine refill?
Go Medications for MedZen app, pick the medicine, click Request Refill, then choose the pharmacy wey you like. The pharmacy go process the request, e go usually take like one day, and dem go notify you when e ready.

## How I fit set medicine reminders?
Go Medications, then Reminders, pick how often you want them remind you, e fit be everyday, two times everyday, or you fit set your own schedule. The reminders go show as push notification for your phone.

## Wetin I go do if my prescription don expire?
Contact your doctor through MedZen app to request renewal. You fit send secure message or book short follow-up visit.

## How I fit move prescription go another pharmacy?
Use the Transfer option wey dey for the medicine inside the app, or contact the pharmacy wey you dey use now. Dem fit send your electronic prescription go the new pharmacy.

## Wetin I go do if I get side effects or drug interactions?
Report any side effect through the app or tell your doctor. If you get question about how your medicines fit dey react with each other, ask your doctor or pharmacist. If na emergency, like serious allergic reaction, contact your local emergency service sharp sharp.
