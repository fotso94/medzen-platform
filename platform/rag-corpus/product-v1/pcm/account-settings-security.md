# Account, Settings and Security (Account, Settings and Security)

## How I fit update my profile for MedZen?
Go to Settings, then Profile, to update your personal information, phone number and email. Make sure your contact details dey current so you go dey receive appointment reminders and result notifications. You fit also add profile picture.

## How I fit turn on two-factor authentication?
Go to Settings, then Security, then Two-Factor Authentication, and follow di steps. MedZen support verification through text message and authenticator apps. Dem dey recommend two-factor authentication for every account, especially for healthcare providers.

## How I fit change my password?
Go to Settings, then Security, then Change Password. Choose password wey reach at least eight characters, mix letters, numbers and symbols, and no use password wey you don already use for another service.

## How I fit control who fit see my health data?
Go to Settings, then Privacy, then Data Sharing. You fit share your records with all your providers, with only some providers, or with nobody at all. Emergency access go still dey available so emergency clinicians fit treat you well well.

## How I fit export or delete my data?
Go to Settings, then Privacy. Export Data go give you full copy of your records, lab results, prescriptions and visit history for PDF or JSON format. Delete Account go request to delete your account; some medical records fit remain for di period wey law require.

## How I fit change notifications?
Go to Settings, then Notifications. You fit manage appointment reminders, lab result alerts, provider messages and medication reminders one by one, separately.

## Which languages MedZen support?
Di MedZen app dey available for English and French, and you fit change di language under Settings, then Language. Di MedZen voice assistant sabi understand and answer for English, French, Kinyarwanda, Nigerian Pidgin and Swahili.
