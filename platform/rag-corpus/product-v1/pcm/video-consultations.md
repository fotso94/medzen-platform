# Video Consultations (Video Meeting with Doctor)

## Wetin I need for video consultation?
You need smartphone, tablet or computer wey get camera and microphone, stable internet connection of at least about 2 megabits per second, and the latest version of MedZen app. Choose one quiet, private, and well-lit place for the call.

## How I fit join my video consultation?
You go get notification small time before the appointment reach. Open MedZen app, go My Appointments, then tap Join Video Call as soon as the button start work, few minutes before the time go start. Give camera and microphone access if dem ask you, then wait for the virtual waiting room until your provider join.

## Wetin dey happen during video consultation?
Your video and audio dey encrypted (dem dey protected). You fit mute your microphone or off your camera anytime you like. The provider fit share their screen make you see results or images. If you gree, dem fit record the consultation for clinical documentation.

## Wetin dey happen after video consultation?
Clinical notes from the visit go enter your Health Records, e go normally take like one day. Any prescription go send electronically go your pharmacy, and you fit book follow-up appointment straight after the call.

## Wetin if my video call get problem?
If video no dey show, check say camera permission dey enabled for your device settings. If sound no dey, check microphone permission and make sure your device no dey on silent. If the quality no clear well, come closer to your Wi-Fi router or use wired connection. If the call cut, go back to the appointment and tap Rejoin; the session go still dey open for some few minutes.

## Which devices and browsers dey work with MedZen video?
MedZen video dey work well well on recent iOS and Android phones and tablets, and on current versions of Chrome, Safari and Edge for web. E better make you get at least about 2 megabits per second for upload and download speed.
