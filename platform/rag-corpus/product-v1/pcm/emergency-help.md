# Emergency Help (Emergency Wahala)

## Wetin I go do if emergency medical case happen?
If somebody get life-threatening problem, call your local emergency number sharp sharp. No depend on this app for life-threatening emergency. Warning signs na chest pain or wahala to breathe, signs of stroke like face wey dey drop for one side, hand wey weak, or wahala to talk well, heavy bleeding wey no wan stop, wen person faint or lose consciousness, serious allergic reaction, if you suspect poison, or serious injury from accident.

## How I fit get urgent help from MedZen?
For urgent problem wey no be life-threatening, open MedZen app, click on Emergency button. Dem go connect you to clinical triage team, dem go check your situation and direct you to the right care.

## How MedZen triage dey work?
First, you go tell the triage nurse wetin dey worry you. Dem go classify your case as emergency, urgent or standard. Emergency cases dem go direct to ambulance or hospital, urgent cases go get appointment same day, standard cases go get the next available slot. The team go follow up with you after urgent or emergency case.

## Wetin I go need ready when I call for urgent help?
Make you ready your MedZen account details like your name and date of birth, the medications wey you dey take now, any allergy wey you know, and description of your symptoms — when e start, how bad e dey from one to ten, and any change wey happen.

## Wetin dey happen after emergency visit?
Dem go record the emergency visit inside your MedZen health records, dem go coordinate follow-up care through the app, and any lab result or imaging from the visit go show for your records.

## How I fit find the emergency room wey close to me?
Click Emergency button for MedZen app to see emergency facilities wey near you. The app go show real-time waiting time wen dem dey available.
