# Privacy and Consent (Privacy and Agreement)

## How MedZen dey protect my health data?
MedZen dey built to protect your health data well well. All message and video call dem encrypt, every time person access your health record, dem dey log am, and na you dey control which providers fit see your data through the privacy settings. You fit also protect your account with two-factor authentication.

## Wetin kind consent MedZen dey ask for?
Dem go ask for treatment consent before any medical procedure or treatment start, and your provider go first explain am to you. Data sharing consent na wetin dey control which providers fit access your record, and you fit change am anytime. Research consent na optional one, e dey allow dem use anonymised data for medical research; you fit opt out anytime you like.

## Wetin be my rights over my data?
You fit see all the data MedZen dey hold about you, ask them to correct anything wrong, request them to delete am (but subject to wetin the law dey require dem to keep), export your data in standard formats, and restrict how dem dey process am. All dis one dey under Settings, then Privacy.

## I fit see who access my records?
Yes. Go to Settings, then Privacy, then Access Log, to see record of everytime person access your health information.

## How dem dey handle my voice assistant data?
When you use MedZen voice assistant, dem go transcribe your recording so dem fit answer your question. Dem go only store your recording and transcript to improve the service if you clearly opt in with the "Help improve MedZen" option, wey dey off by default. Abeg no share sensitive personal or medical details for any recording.
