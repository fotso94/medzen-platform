# Bout MylesTech Solutions

## Wetin be MylesTech Solutions?
MylesTech Solutions na tech company wey dey build digital solutions for Africa and beyond, with di motto "Empowering Africa, one digital solution at a time". Dia work dey cover healthcare innovation, education technology, financial technology, real estate technology, transport and mobility, cloud and DevOps engineering, and change management consulting. Di product wey dem sabi pass na di MedZen Health telemedicine platform.

## Wetin be MylesTech mission and vision?
Di mission of MylesTech Solutions na to empower healthier lives and more innovative futures through virtual care, digital innovation and world-class education, starting from Africa. Dia vision na to become Africa leading force for digital transformation, so dat technology go dey available for everybody.

## Wetin be MylesTech core values?
MylesTech Solutions get six core values: innovation first, access for all, excellence in execution, trust and transparency, empowerment through knowledge, and global thinking with local action.

## Who dey lead MylesTech Solutions?
MylesTech Solutions dey led by Alain Bagmi, founder and chief executive officer, Mukum Ngwa, co-founder and chief operating officer, and Mandela Akah, chief technology officer.

## Wetin be di services wey MylesTech dey offer?
MylesTech dey offer seven service areas: healthcare innovation like telemedicine platforms and electronic health records, EdTech and online learning platforms, FinTech and digital payment applications, real estate technology, transport and mobility software, technology and infrastructure consulting wey include agile methods, networking, cloud computing and DevOps, and change management consulting for digital transformation.

## Wetin be di oda products wey MylesTech dey make?
Apart from di MedZen Health products, MylesTech dey make Clockwise, na intelligent workforce management and scheduling platform for enterprise teams. MylesTech also dey run one training bootcamp wey dey teach practical technology skills.
