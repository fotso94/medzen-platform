# Health Records (Health Papers) and Lab Results

## How I fit see my lab results for MedZen?
Open MedZen app, go to Health Records, then Lab Results. Results dey listed by date, the latest one dey first. Tap any result to see the detailed values and the reference ranges.

## Wetin the lab result statuses mean?
Pending mean say dem don collect the sample but dem never process am. In progress mean say the laboratory dey process am. Completed mean say your results don ready. Normal mean say all the values dey inside the expected range. Abnormal mean say one or more values dey outside the normal range, and your provider go contact you.

## Wetin be the common lab tests?
Complete blood count dey measure red and white blood cells, haemoglobin and platelets. Basic metabolic panel dey check blood sugar, calcium, electrolytes and kidney work. Lipid panel dey measure cholesterol and triglycerides. Thyroid panel dey check thyroid work. HbA1c dey show average blood sugar for like three months, and dem dey use am to monitor diabetes. Urinalysis dey screen for kidney, liver and metabolic problems.

## How long lab results dey take?
Routine blood work dey normally take one to two days. Special tests fit take several working days, pathology fit take about one week or more, and genetic tests fit take some weeks. Your app go show the status of each test.

## Wetin I go do if my lab results no normal?
Dem go notify your provider automatically, and dem fit call or message you to explain the next steps. Plenty abnormal values no serious and dem easy to manage, so no panic. Follow up with your provider as dem recommend, and use the app to book visit if e dey needed.

## Where I go see my medical history and visit notes?
Go Health Records for MedZen app. You go see your visit history, clinical notes, prescriptions and lab results all for one place, and you fit request export of your full record from the privacy settings.
