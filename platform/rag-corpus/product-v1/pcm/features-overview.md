# MedZen Features Overview (Wetin MedZen Fit Do - Overview)

## Wetin be di main features of MedZen patient app?
MedZen patient app dey give you HD video consultation wit healthcare providers, smart scheduling wit real-time availability and reminders, electronic prescriptions wey dey connect straight to pharmacy, secure messaging wit your care team, provider directory wit reviews, on-demand and scheduled care, digital health records, lab results, medication management and one emergency button.

## Wetin be digital health records for MedZen?
Your MedZen health record dey keep your medical history, visit notes, prescriptions, lab results and clinical notes for one place. You fit check dem anytime for di app under Health Records (Health Records), and na you go decide which providers fit see your data through di privacy settings.

## Wetin be smart scheduling?
Smart scheduling dey make you see provider real-time availability, book one slot with small tap, receive reminders before di visit, and add di appointment to your calendar. You fit also reschedule or cancel from My Appointments (My Appointments).

## Wetin be electronic prescriptions?
After consultation, your provider fit send digital prescription straight go your preferred pharmacy from MedZen. You fit see all your active prescriptions, dosages and refill status under Medications for di app.

## Wetin be secure messaging?
Secure messaging dey make you send and receive message wit your care team inside di app. Di messages dey encrypted, and you go get notification when provider reply you.

## Wetin be di tools MedZen dey give healthcare providers?
Providers dey get dashboard wey show today appointments, pending lab results and messages wey no read yet, patient lists wit full histories, AI-assisted clinical note generation, electronic prescribing wit interaction warnings, lab ordering, video consultations and schedule management. Dis tools na part of MedzenHealth Pro.

## Wetin facility administrators fit do for MedZen?
Facility administrators fit manage healthcare provider profiles and schedules, set up and manage departments, and monitor patient flow and care metrics for dia facility.
