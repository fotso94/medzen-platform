# MedzenHealth Caregiver

## Wetin be MedzenHealth Caregiver?
MedzenHealth Caregiver na special MedZen platform wey dem make for home caregivers. E dey help caregivers coordinate patient care, follow up on appointments and medications, and check the health of the person wey dem dey take care of.

## Who MedzenHealth Caregiver dey for?
E dey for family members and professional caregivers wey dey take care of patient for house and wey need to stay connected with the patient healthcare providers through MedZen.
