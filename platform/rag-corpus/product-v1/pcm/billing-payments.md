# Billing and Payments (Billing and Payment)

## How much consultation or video consultation dey cost on MedZen?
Na each provider or care center dey set the price for consultation, video consultation, follow-up or specialist visit, no be MedZen, and e dey change depending on the type of visit. The exact price go show for the app before you confirm any booking, so you go always know the price before you pay.

## Which payment methods I fit use?
The payment options wey dey available for you go show for checkout inside the MedZen app. E depend on your country and the provider. Some providers still dey accept payment for clinic when na in-person visit.

## I fit use my health insurance with MedZen?
If your insurer dey supported, you fit add your insurance details for Settings (Setting), then Insurance, and visits wey qualify fit dey billed to the insurer. If no be so, you go pay for the visit yourself and you fit use the detailed receipt from the app to claim your money back.

## Where I go see my invoices and receipts?
Go to Finance, then Invoices, inside the MedZen app. Every visit go generate invoice wey show the services and the amount, and you fit download am as PDF for your record or to use claim insurance.

## Who I go contact if I get billing question?
Use the support or messaging options wey dey inside the MedZen app and explain the invoice or payment wey concern. You fit also contact MylesTech Solutions through email at info@mylestechsolutionsllc.com.
