# For Healthcare Providers

## How healthcare professionals dey use MedZen?
Healthcare professionals dey use MedzenHealth Pro, wey dey available at pro.medzenhealth.app, to manage patients, do video consultation, document visits and prescribe medicine electronically. Facility administrators dey use am to manage staff, departments and how patients dey flow.

## Wetin dey on the provider dashboard?
The provider dashboard dey show today appointments and schedule, lab results wey still dey wait for review, patient messages wey no read yet, and clinical notes wey dey wait for signature.

## How providers dey manage patients for MedZen?
Providers go see list of patients wey dem assign to dem with the latest visit information, dem fit open patient profile with full medical history, medications, allergies and old notes, and dem fit search for patients by name, identifier or phone number.

## How clinical notes dey work for MedZen Pro?
MedZen Pro fit generate draft SOAP note from video consultation transcript with AI assistance, e get templates for common visit types, e go save drafts automatically, and provider fit sign the note digitally with time stamp and provider identifier.

## How electronic prescribing dey work?
Providers go search the medication database, set the dose, how often to take am, how long, and number of refill, dem go get interaction warnings, and dem fit send the prescription straight go patient preferred pharmacy.

## How providers dey order lab tests?
Common lab panels fit order with one tap and custom tests fit select too. Results go show for provider dashboard when dem ready, and any abnormal value go dey flag for priority review.

## How providers dey manage their schedule?
Providers go set their available hours under Settings, then Schedule, and dem fit block time for lunch, meetings or admin work. Patients fit only book appointment during the time wey provider dey available.
