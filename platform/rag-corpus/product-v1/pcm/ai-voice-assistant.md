# MedZen Voice Assistant

## Wetin be MedZen voice assistant?
MedZen voice assistant dey allow you ask question by talk. E go listen to your recording, write down wetin you talk, find relevant information, then answer you for text and for spoken voice. E fit explain MedZen features and help you understand general health information.

## Which languages the voice assistant dey understand?
The voice assistant dey understand and answer for English, French, Kinyarwanda, Nigerian Pidgin and Swahili. Choose your language before you record so that the assistant go listen for the correct language.

## How I fit use the voice assistant?
Select your language, tap the microphone button, ask your question clear-clear, then tap stop. The assistant go show the transcript of wetin you talk, write im answer, then play the answer for voice. Tap New conversation to start again for another topic.

## Wetin the voice assistant fit help with?
E fit explain how to use MedZen, like booking, video calls, prescriptions, lab results and Settings, and e fit give general health information. If e use MedZen documents, the answer go dey marked as grounded and go show im sources. If not, e go answer from general knowledge and e go tell you say na so.

## The voice assistant fit diagnose me or take place of doctor?
No. The assistant dey give general information only. E no dey diagnose, prescribe medicine, or take the place of professional medical advice. If you need personal medical advice, book consultation with a provider, and if e be emergency, call your local emergency number.

## The voice assistant dey remember our conversation?
Inside one conversation, the assistant go remember your recent questions so you fit ask follow-up questions. If you tap New conversation or change the language, e go clear this memory. Recordings dey kept only to improve the service if you agree to am (opt in).
