# About MedZen Health

## Wetin be MedZen Health?
MedZen Health na telemedicine and digital health platform wey MylesTech Solutions build. E dey connect patients with doctors, nurses and other healthcare providers so you fit get care from house, from your phone or computer. MedZen dey designed for Africa, including remote communities, and na only basic phone with simple text message or internet connection e need for many services.

## Which MedZen products dey?
Three MedZen products dey. MedzenHealth na the patient app for booking appointments, video consultations, health records and messaging. MedzenHealth Pro dey give healthcare professionals advanced clinical tools, including AI-assisted documentation. MedzenHealth Caregiver na dedicated app for home caregivers to coordinate patient care and monitor health.

## Where I fit use MedZen?
MedZen dey available as web application for medzenhealth.app and as mobile app for iOS and Android. Healthcare professionals dey sign in to the professional platform for pro.medzenhealth.app. The platform dey built for patients and providers across Africa.

## Wetin MedZen dey offer patients?
Patients fit book in-person or video appointments, hold HD video consultations, receive electronic prescriptions wey dem send direct to pharmacy, message their care team securely, keep digital health records, view lab results, manage medications and reminders, find nearby Care Centers (Care Centers) and providers, read provider reviews, and reach Emergency (Emergency) help quick from the app.

## Who dey build MedZen Health?
MedZen Health na MylesTech Solutions build am, na technology company wey their mission be to empower healthier lives and more innovative futures through virtual care, digital innovation and education, starting from Africa. MylesTech also dey build electronic health record, education, payment and workforce software.
