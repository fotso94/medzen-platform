# Emergency Help

## What should I do in a medical emergency?
If someone has a life-threatening problem, call your local emergency number immediately. Do not rely on the app for a life-threatening emergency. Warning signs include chest pain or difficulty breathing, signs of a stroke such as a drooping face, arm weakness or trouble speaking, severe bleeding that will not stop, loss of consciousness, a severe allergic reaction, suspected poisoning, or serious injuries from an accident.

## How do I get urgent help from MedZen?
For urgent problems that are not life-threatening, open the MedZen app and tap the Emergency button. You are connected to the clinical triage team, who assess your situation and direct you to the right care.

## How does MedZen triage work?
First you describe your symptoms to the triage nurse. Your case is classified as emergency, urgent or standard. Emergency cases are directed to an ambulance or hospital, urgent cases get a same-day appointment, and standard cases get the next available slot. The team follows up with you after an urgent or emergency encounter.

## What should I have ready when I call for urgent help?
Have your MedZen account details such as your name and date of birth, your current list of medications, any known allergies, and a description of your symptoms, including when they started, how severe they are on a scale of one to ten, and any changes.

## What happens after an emergency visit?
The emergency visit is recorded in your MedZen health records, follow-up care is coordinated through the app, and any lab results or imaging from the visit become available in your records.

## How do I find the nearest emergency room?
Tap the Emergency button in the MedZen app to see nearby emergency facilities. The app shows real-time waiting times when they are available.
