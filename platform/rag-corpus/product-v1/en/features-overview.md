# MedZen Features Overview

## What are the main features of the MedZen patient app?
The MedZen patient app offers HD video consultations with healthcare providers, smart scheduling with real-time availability and reminders, electronic prescriptions with direct pharmacy integration, secure messaging with your care team, a provider directory with reviews, on-demand and scheduled care, digital health records, lab results, medication management and an emergency button.

## What are digital health records in MedZen?
Your MedZen health record keeps your medical history, visit notes, prescriptions, lab results and clinical notes in one place. You can review them any time in the app under Health Records, and you decide which providers can see your data through the privacy settings.

## What is smart scheduling?
Smart scheduling lets you see a provider's real-time availability, book a slot in a few taps, get reminders before the visit, and add the appointment to your calendar. You can also reschedule or cancel from My Appointments.

## What are electronic prescriptions?
After a consultation, your provider can send a digital prescription directly to your preferred pharmacy from MedZen. You can see all your active prescriptions, dosages and refill status under Medications in the app.

## What is secure messaging?
Secure messaging lets you send and receive messages with your care team inside the app. Messages are encrypted, and you get a notification when a provider replies.

## What tools does MedZen give healthcare providers?
Providers get a dashboard with the day's appointments, pending lab results and unread messages, patient lists with full histories, AI-assisted clinical note generation, electronic prescribing with interaction warnings, lab ordering, video consultations and schedule management. These tools are part of MedzenHealth Pro.

## What can facility administrators do in MedZen?
Facility administrators can manage healthcare provider profiles and schedules, set up and manage departments, and monitor patient flow and care metrics for their facility.
