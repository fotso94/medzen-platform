# Health Records and Lab Results

## How do I see my lab results in MedZen?
Open the MedZen app and go to Health Records, then Lab Results. Results are listed by date with the most recent first. Tap a result to see the detailed values and the reference ranges.

## What do the lab result statuses mean?
Pending means the sample was collected and is waiting to be processed. In progress means the laboratory is processing it. Completed means your results are ready. Normal means all values are within the expected range. Abnormal means one or more values are outside the normal range and your provider will contact you.

## What are the most common lab tests?
A complete blood count measures red and white blood cells, haemoglobin and platelets. A basic metabolic panel checks blood sugar, calcium, electrolytes and kidney function. A lipid panel measures cholesterol and triglycerides. A thyroid panel checks thyroid function. HbA1c shows average blood sugar over about three months and is used for diabetes monitoring. A urinalysis screens for kidney, liver and metabolic problems.

## How long do lab results take?
Routine blood work usually takes one to two days. Specialised tests can take several business days, pathology about a week or more, and genetic tests a few weeks. Your app shows the status of each test.

## What should I do if my lab results are abnormal?
Your provider is notified automatically and may call or message you to explain the next steps. Many abnormal values are minor and easy to manage, so do not panic. Follow up with your provider as recommended, and use the app to book a visit if needed.

## Where do I find my medical history and visit notes?
Go to Health Records in the MedZen app. You will find your visit history, clinical notes, prescriptions and lab results in one place, and you can request an export of your full record from the privacy settings.
