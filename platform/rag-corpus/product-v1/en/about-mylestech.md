# About MylesTech Solutions

## What is MylesTech Solutions?
MylesTech Solutions is a technology company that builds digital solutions for Africa and beyond, with the motto "Empowering Africa, one digital solution at a time". Its work covers healthcare innovation, education technology, financial technology, real estate technology, transport and mobility, cloud and DevOps engineering, and change management consulting. Its best-known product is the MedZen Health telemedicine platform.

## What are MylesTech's mission and vision?
The mission of MylesTech Solutions is to empower healthier lives and more innovative futures through virtual care, digital innovation and world-class education, starting in Africa. Its vision is to become Africa's leading force for digital transformation, making technology available to everyone.

## What are MylesTech's core values?
MylesTech Solutions has six core values: innovation first, access for all, excellence in execution, trust and transparency, empowerment through knowledge, and global thinking with local action.

## Who leads MylesTech Solutions?
MylesTech Solutions is led by Alain Bagmi, founder and chief executive officer, Mukum Ngwa, co-founder and chief operating officer, and Mandela Akah, chief technology officer.

## What services does MylesTech offer?
MylesTech offers seven service areas: healthcare innovation such as telemedicine platforms and electronic health records, EdTech and online learning platforms, FinTech and digital payment applications, real estate technology, transport and mobility software, technology and infrastructure consulting including agile methods, networking, cloud computing and DevOps, and change management consulting for digital transformation.

## What other products does MylesTech make?
Besides the MedZen Health products, MylesTech makes Clockwise, an intelligent workforce management and scheduling platform for enterprise teams. MylesTech also runs a training bootcamp that teaches practical technology skills.
