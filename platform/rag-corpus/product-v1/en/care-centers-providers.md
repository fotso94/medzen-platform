# Care Centers and Providers

## How do I find a care center near me?
Open the MedZen app and go to Care Centers in the bottom navigation. Search by name, specialty or location, or use the map view to see nearby facilities.

## How can I filter care centers?
You can filter by distance, for example within five, ten, twenty-five or fifty kilometres, by specialty such as general practice, cardiology, dermatology or paediatrics, by same-day availability, by patient rating, and by accepted insurance.

## What information does a care center listing show?
Each listing shows the name, address and contact details, opening hours, departments and specialties, patient ratings and reviews, distance from you, accepted insurance providers and available appointment slots.

## How do I book at a care center?
Select the care center, choose a department or provider, pick an available time slot and confirm. The booking then appears under My Appointments.

## How do I find and choose a doctor?
Use the provider directory in the MedZen app to browse doctors, nurses and specialists with their profiles, specialties, availability and verified patient reviews. You can also let MedZen match you with a suitable provider when you book.

## Can I get care immediately?
Yes. MedZen offers on-demand consultations when you need care right away, as well as scheduled appointments at a time that suits you.
