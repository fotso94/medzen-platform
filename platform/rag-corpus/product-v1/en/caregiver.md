# MedzenHealth Caregiver

## What is MedzenHealth Caregiver?
MedzenHealth Caregiver is a dedicated MedZen platform for home caregivers. It helps caregivers coordinate a patient's care, keep track of appointments and medications, and monitor the health of the person they care for.

## Who is MedzenHealth Caregiver for?
It is for family members and professional caregivers who look after a patient at home and need to stay coordinated with the patient's healthcare providers through MedZen.
