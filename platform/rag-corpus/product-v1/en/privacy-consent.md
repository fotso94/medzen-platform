# Privacy and Consent

## How does MedZen protect my health data?
MedZen is designed to protect your health data. Messages and video calls are encrypted, every access to your health record is logged, and you control which providers can see your data through the privacy settings. You can also protect your account with two-factor authentication.

## What kinds of consent does MedZen ask for?
Treatment consent is requested before a medical procedure or treatment, and your provider explains it first. Data sharing consent controls which providers can access your record, and you can change it at any time. Research consent is optional and lets anonymised data be used for medical research; you can opt out whenever you want.

## What are my rights over my data?
You can view all the data MedZen holds about you, ask for corrections, request deletion subject to legal retention rules, export your data in standard formats, and restrict how it is processed. These options are under Settings, then Privacy.

## Can I see who accessed my records?
Yes. Go to Settings, then Privacy, then Access Log, to see a record of every access to your health information.

## How is my voice assistant data handled?
When you use the MedZen voice assistant, your recording is transcribed to answer your question. Your recording and transcript are only stored to improve the service if you explicitly opt in with the "Help improve MedZen" option, which is off by default. Please avoid sharing sensitive personal or medical details in a recording.
