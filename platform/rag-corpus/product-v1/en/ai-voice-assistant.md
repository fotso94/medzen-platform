# The MedZen Voice Assistant

## What is the MedZen voice assistant?
The MedZen voice assistant lets you ask questions by speaking. It listens to your recording, writes down what you said, finds relevant information, and answers you in text and in a spoken voice. It can explain MedZen features and help you understand general health information.

## Which languages does the voice assistant understand?
The voice assistant understands and answers in English, French, Kinyarwanda, Nigerian Pidgin and Swahili. Choose your language before recording so that the assistant listens in the right language.

## How do I use the voice assistant?
Select your language, tap the microphone button, ask your question clearly, then tap stop. The assistant shows the transcript of what you said, writes its answer, and plays the answer aloud. Tap New conversation to start again on a different topic.

## What can the voice assistant help with?
It can explain how to use MedZen, such as booking, video calls, prescriptions, lab results and settings, and it can give general health information. When it uses MedZen documents, the answer is marked as grounded and shows its sources. Otherwise it answers from general knowledge and says so.

## Can the voice assistant diagnose me or replace a doctor?
No. The assistant gives general information only. It does not diagnose, prescribe or replace professional medical advice. For personal medical advice, book a consultation with a provider, and in an emergency call your local emergency number.

## Does the voice assistant remember our conversation?
Within one conversation, the assistant remembers your recent questions so you can ask follow-up questions. Tapping New conversation or changing the language clears this memory. Recordings are only kept to improve the service if you opt in.
