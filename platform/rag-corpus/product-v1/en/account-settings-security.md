# Account, Settings and Security

## How do I update my profile in MedZen?
Go to Settings, then Profile, to update your personal information, phone number and email. Keep your contact details current so you receive appointment reminders and results notifications. You can also add a profile picture.

## How do I turn on two-factor authentication?
Go to Settings, then Security, then Two-Factor Authentication, and follow the steps. MedZen supports verification by text message and authenticator apps. Two-factor authentication is recommended for every account and especially for healthcare providers.

## How do I change my password?
Go to Settings, then Security, then Change Password. Choose a password of at least eight characters that mixes letters, numbers and symbols, and do not reuse a password from another service.

## How do I control who can see my health data?
Go to Settings, then Privacy, then Data Sharing. You can share your records with all your providers, with selected providers only, or with none. Emergency access remains available so that emergency clinicians can treat you safely.

## How do I export or delete my data?
Go to Settings, then Privacy. Export Data gives you a full copy of your records, lab results, prescriptions and visit history in PDF or JSON format. Delete Account requests deletion of your account; some medical records may be kept for the period required by law.

## How do I change notifications?
Go to Settings, then Notifications. You can manage appointment reminders, lab result alerts, provider messages and medication reminders separately.

## Which languages does MedZen support?
The MedZen app is available in English and French, and you can change the language under Settings, then Language. The MedZen voice assistant understands and answers in English, French, Kinyarwanda, Nigerian Pidgin and Swahili.
