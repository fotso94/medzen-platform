# Medications and Prescriptions

## Where can I see my prescriptions?
Open the MedZen app and go to Health Records, then Medications. Each prescription shows the medication name, dose, how often to take it, the prescribing doctor and the refill status.

## How do I request a prescription refill?
Go to Medications in the MedZen app, select the medication and tap Request Refill, then choose your preferred pharmacy. The pharmacy processes the request, usually within about a day, and you are notified when it is ready.

## How do I set medication reminders?
Go to Medications, then Reminders, and choose how often you want to be reminded, for example daily, twice daily or a custom schedule. Reminders arrive as push notifications on your device.

## What if my prescription has expired?
Contact your provider through the MedZen app to request a renewal. You can send a secure message or book a short follow-up visit.

## How do I transfer a prescription to another pharmacy?
Use the Transfer option on the medication in the app, or contact your current pharmacy. Your electronic prescription can be sent to the new pharmacy.

## What should I do about side effects or drug interactions?
Report side effects through the app or tell your provider. For questions about interactions between your medications, ask your provider or pharmacist. In an emergency, such as a severe allergic reaction, contact your local emergency services immediately.
