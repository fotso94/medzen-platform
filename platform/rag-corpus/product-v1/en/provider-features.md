# For Healthcare Providers

## How do healthcare professionals use MedZen?
Healthcare professionals use MedzenHealth Pro, available at pro.medzenhealth.app, to manage patients, consult by video, document visits and prescribe electronically. Facility administrators use it to manage staff, departments and patient flow.

## What is on the provider dashboard?
The provider dashboard shows today's appointments and schedule, pending lab results that need review, unread patient messages and clinical notes waiting for a signature.

## How do providers manage patients in MedZen?
Providers see a list of their assigned patients with the latest visit information, open a patient profile with full medical history, medications, allergies and past notes, and search for patients by name, identifier or phone number.

## How do clinical notes work in MedZen Pro?
MedZen Pro can generate a draft SOAP note from a video consultation transcript with AI assistance, offers templates for common visit types, saves drafts automatically, and lets the provider sign the note digitally with a timestamp and provider identifier.

## How does electronic prescribing work?
Providers search the medication database, set the dose, frequency, duration and refill count, receive interaction warnings, and send the prescription directly to the patient's preferred pharmacy.

## How do providers order lab tests?
Common lab panels can be ordered with one tap and custom tests can be selected. Results are delivered to the provider dashboard when ready, and abnormal values are flagged for priority review.

## How do providers manage their schedule?
Providers set their available hours under Settings, then Schedule, and can block time for lunch, meetings or administration. Patients can only book during the provider's available slots.
