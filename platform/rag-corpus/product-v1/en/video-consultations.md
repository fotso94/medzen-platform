# Video Consultations

## What do I need for a video consultation?
You need a smartphone, tablet or computer with a camera and microphone, a stable internet connection of at least about 2 megabits per second, and the latest version of the MedZen app. Choose a quiet, private, well-lit place for the call.

## How do I join my video consultation?
You get a notification shortly before the appointment. Open the MedZen app, go to My Appointments and tap Join Video Call when the button becomes active a few minutes before the start. Allow camera and microphone access if asked, then wait in the virtual waiting room until your provider joins.

## What happens during a video consultation?
Your video and audio are encrypted. You can mute your microphone or turn your camera off at any time. The provider may share their screen to show you results or images. With your consent, the consultation may be recorded for clinical documentation.

## What happens after a video consultation?
Clinical notes from the visit are added to your health records, usually within a day. Any prescriptions are sent electronically to your pharmacy, and you can book a follow-up directly after the call.

## What if my video call has problems?
If there is no video, check that camera permission is enabled in your device settings. If there is no sound, check microphone permission and that your device is not on silent. For poor quality, move closer to your Wi-Fi router or use a wired connection. If the call drops, return to the appointment and tap Rejoin; the session stays open for a few minutes.

## Which devices and browsers work with MedZen video?
MedZen video works on recent iOS and Android phones and tablets, and in current versions of Chrome, Safari and Edge on the web. A minimum of about 2 megabits per second upload and download speed is recommended.
