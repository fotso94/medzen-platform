# Booking Appointments

## How do I book an appointment in MedZen?
Open the MedZen app and go to Appointments, then choose Book New Appointment. Pick the appointment type, such as in-person, video consultation or follow-up, select a healthcare provider or let MedZen match you with one, choose an available date and time, and confirm. You will receive a confirmation and reminders before the visit.

## What types of appointments can I book?
You can book a general consultation, a video consultation, a follow-up visit, a specialist consultation, lab work such as a blood draw, or an emergency walk-in visit. Video consultations are usually shorter than in-person visits, and specialist consultations are usually longer. The exact duration is shown when you book.

## How do I reschedule an appointment?
Go to My Appointments in the MedZen app, select the appointment and tap Reschedule, then choose a new time. Rescheduling is possible up to a short time before the appointment, and the app tells you the cut-off for your provider.

## How do I cancel an appointment, and is there a fee?
Go to My Appointments, select the appointment and tap Cancel. Cancelling well in advance, normally at least 24 hours before, is free. Late cancellations and missed appointments may be charged by the provider according to the policy shown when you booked.

## What happens if I miss my appointment?
If you miss an appointment without cancelling, the provider may charge a no-show fee according to the policy shown at booking. You can book a new appointment at any time from the Appointments screen.

## When are providers available?
Availability depends on each provider and care center, and only free slots are shown when you book. Some providers offer evening or weekend slots. For urgent problems outside normal hours, use the Emergency button in the app or contact your local emergency services.
