# About MedZen Health

## What is MedZen Health?
MedZen Health is a telemedicine and digital health platform built by MylesTech Solutions. It connects patients with doctors, nurses and other healthcare providers so you can get care from home, on your phone or computer. MedZen is designed for Africa, including remote communities, and only needs a basic phone and a simple text message or internet connection for many services.

## Which MedZen products exist?
There are three MedZen products. MedzenHealth is the patient app for booking appointments, video consultations, health records and messaging. MedzenHealth Pro gives healthcare professionals advanced clinical tools, including AI-assisted documentation. MedzenHealth Caregiver is a dedicated app for home caregivers to coordinate patient care and monitor health.

## Where can I use MedZen?
MedZen is available as a web application at medzenhealth.app and as a mobile app for iOS and Android. Healthcare professionals sign in to the professional platform at pro.medzenhealth.app. The platform is built for patients and providers across Africa.

## What does MedZen offer patients?
Patients can book in-person or video appointments, hold HD video consultations, receive electronic prescriptions sent directly to a pharmacy, message their care team securely, keep digital health records, view lab results, manage medications and reminders, find nearby care centers and providers, read provider reviews, and reach emergency help quickly from the app.

## Who builds MedZen Health?
MedZen Health is built by MylesTech Solutions, a technology company whose mission is to empower healthier lives and more innovative futures through virtual care, digital innovation and education, starting in Africa. MylesTech also builds electronic health record, education, payment and workforce software.
