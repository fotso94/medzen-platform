# Billing and Payments

## How much does a consultation or video consultation cost on MedZen?
The price of a consultation, a video consultation, a follow-up or a specialist visit is set by each provider or care center, not by MedZen, and it varies by the type of visit. The exact fee is shown in the app before you confirm a booking, so you always know the price in advance.

## Which payment methods can I use?
The payment options available to you are shown at checkout in the MedZen app. They depend on your country and the provider. Some providers also accept payment at the clinic for in-person visits.

## Can I use my health insurance with MedZen?
If your insurer is supported, you can add your insurance details under Settings, then Insurance, and eligible visits can be billed to the insurer. Otherwise you pay for the visit and can use the itemised receipt from the app to claim reimbursement.

## Where do I find my invoices and receipts?
Go to Finance, then Invoices, in the MedZen app. Every visit produces an invoice with the services and amounts, and you can download it as a PDF for your records or for an insurance claim.

## Who do I contact about a billing question?
Use the support or messaging options in the MedZen app and describe the invoice or payment concerned. You can also contact MylesTech Solutions by email at info@mylestechsolutionsllc.com.
