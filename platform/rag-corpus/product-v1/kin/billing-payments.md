# Kwishyura no Kwakira Amafaranga

## Ikiguzi cy'ubujyanama cyangwa ubujyanama bwa videwo kuri MedZen ni angahe?
Ikiguzi cy'ubujyanama, ubujyanama bwa videwo, isuzuma ry'ikurikira cyangwa gusura umuganga wihariye gishyirwaho na buri mutanga serivisi cyangwa ikigo cy'ubuvuzi, ntabwo gishyirwaho na MedZen, kandi kirahindagurika bitewe n'ubwoko bw'isuzuma. Ikiguzi nyakuri kigaragazwa muri porogaramu mbere y'uko wemeza gufata gahunda, ku buryo umenya igiciro mbere y'igihe.

## Ni izihe nzira z'ubwishyu nshobora gukoresha?
Uburyo bw'ubwishyu bushobora kugukorera bugaragazwa igihe usoza gutanga gahunda muri porogaramu ya MedZen. Bishingiye ku gihugu ukiriyo n'uwatanga serivisi. Abatanga serivisi bamwe na bamwe nabo bemera ko wishyura ku kigo cy'ubuvuzi igihe ugiye kwisuzumisha ubwawe aho.

## Nshobora gukoresha ubwishingizi bwanjye bw'ubuzima kuri MedZen?
Niba ubwishingizi bwawe bushyigikiwe, ushobora kongeramo amakuru y'ubwishingizi bwawe munsi ya Settings (Igenamiterere), hanyuma ukajya kuri Insurance (Ubwishingizi), kandi isuzuma rikwiye rishobora kwishyurwa n'ubwishingizi. Bitabaye ibyo, wishyura wowe ubwawe isuzuma, hanyuma ukoresha inyemezabuguzi irambuye iva muri porogaramu kugira ngo usabe kwishyurwa.

## Nabona he inyemezabuguzi n'inyemezabwishyu zanjye?
Jya kuri Finance (Imari), hanyuma ukajya kuri Invoices (Inyemezabuguzi), muri porogaramu ya MedZen. Buri isuzuma ritanga inyemezabuguzi igaragaza serivisi zatanzwe n'amafaranga yishyuwe, kandi ushobora kuyimanura mu buryo bwa PDF kugira ngo uyibike cyangwa uyikoreshe mu gusaba kwishyurwa n'ubwishingizi.

## Ni nde nabaza ku kibazo cyerekeye kwishyura?
Koresha uburyo bwo gufashwa cyangwa kohereza ubutumwa muri porogaramu ya MedZen, hanyuma usobanure inyemezabuguzi cyangwa ubwishyu bireba. Ushobora na none guhamagara cyangwa kwandikira MylesTech Solutions kuri imeyili: info@mylestechsolutionsllc.com.
