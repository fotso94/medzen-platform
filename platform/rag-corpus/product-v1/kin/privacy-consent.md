# Ubwigenge n'Uburenganzira ku Makuru ndetse n'Kwemera

## MedZen irinda ite amakuru yanjye y'ubuzima?
MedZen yakozwe kugira ngo irinde amakuru yawe y'ubuzima. Ubutumwa n'amahamagara ya video birinzwe (encrypted), buri gukoresha dosiye yawe y'ubuzima birandikwa, kandi ugenzura abaganga bashobora kubona amakuru yawe binyuze mu igenamiterere ry'Privacy (Ubwigenge). Ushobora kandi kurinda konti yawe ukoresheje uburyo bw'ibyiciro bibiri byo kwemeza (two-factor authentication).

## Ni ubuhe bwoko bw'uburenganzira MedZen isaba kugira ngo yemererwe?
Uburenganzira bwo kwemera ubuvuzi busabwa mbere y'uko igikorwa cy'ubuvuzi cyangwa umuti utangwa, kandi umuganga wawe abisobanura mbere. Uburenganzira bwo gusangira amakuru bugenzura abaganga bashobora kubona dosiye yawe, kandi ushobora kubuhindura igihe cyose ubishatse. Uburenganzira bwo gukoreshwa mu bushakashatsi ni ubushake, kandi bwemerera ko amakuru atagaragaza uwo ariwe akoreshwa mu bushakashatsi bw'ubuvuzi; ushobora kubireka igihe cyose ubishaka.

## Ni ubuhe burenganzira mfite ku makuru yanjye?
Ushobora kureba amakuru yose MedZen ifite kuri wowe, gusaba ko ahinduka niba hari ikosa, gusaba ko asibwa uko amategeko abiteganya abyemeza, kubyimura mu buryo busanzwe bukoreshwa, no kugenzura uburyo akoreshwamo. Ibi bikorerwa muri Settings (Igenamiterere), hanyuma Privacy (Ubwigenge).

## Nshobora kureba abagejeje amaso kuri dosiye yanjye?
Yego. Jya muri Settings (Igenamiterere), hanyuma Privacy (Ubwigenge), hanyuma Access Log (Urutonde rw'uwagejeje amaso), kugira ngo urebe urutonde rw'ubwose bwagejeje amaso ku makuru yawe y'ubuzima.

## Amakuru y'umufasha w'ijwi (voice assistant) akoreshwa gute?
Igihe ukoresheje umufasha w'ijwi wa MedZen, ijwi ryawe ryanditswe kugira ngo hasubizwe ikibazo cyawe. Ijwi ryawe n'inyandiko yaryo bibikwa gusa kugira ngo serivisi inozwe niba wemeye ubushake bwawe binyuze ku bwifuzo "Help improve MedZen" ("Fasha MedZen kunoza serivisi"), buri buhagaze ku muzenguruko utari bwo. Twagusaba kutavuga amakuru y'ibanga bwite cyangwa ay'ubuvuzi mu ijwi ryawe.
