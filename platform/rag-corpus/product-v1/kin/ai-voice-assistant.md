# Umufasha w'Ijwi wa MedZen

## Umufasha w'ijwi wa MedZen ni iki?
Umufasha w'ijwi wa MedZen ukwemerera kubaza ibibazo mu magambo. Wumva ijwi ryawe, wandika ibyo wavuze, ukashaka amakuru ajyanye n'ikibazo cyawe, hanyuma akaguha igisubizo mu nyandiko no mu ijwi. Ushobora gusobanura ibyo MedZen ishoboye gukora no kugufasha gusobanukirwa amakuru rusange y'ubuzima.

## Umufasha w'ijwi wumva indimi ki?
Umufasha w'ijwi wumva kandi asubiza mu Cyongereza, Igifaransa, Ikinyarwanda, Nigerian Pidgin, n'Igiswahili. Hitamo ururimi mbere yo kwandika ijwi ryawe kugira ngo umufasha akumve mu rurimi rukwiye.

## Ese nkoresha ute umufasha w'ijwi?
Hitamo ururimi rwawe, ukande buto y'mikoro, ubaze ikibazo cyawe mu magambo asobanutse, hanyuma ukande stop. Umufasha uzagaragaza inyandiko y'ibyo wavuze, akandika igisubizo cye, hanyuma akakigusomera. Kanda New conversation (Ikiganiro gishya) kugira ngo utangire ikindi kiganiro ku kindi kibazo.

## Umufasha w'ijwi ashobora kugufasha kuki?
Ashobora gusobanura uburyo bwo gukoresha MedZen, urugero nko gufata gahunda, guhamagara kuri video, imiti, ibisubizo by'ibizamini, na settings (igenamiterere). Ashobora kandi kuguha amakuru rusange ku buzima. Igihe akoresheje inyandiko za MedZen, igisubizo kiba cyerekanwe ko cyashingiye ku byo dufite (grounded), kikanagaragaza inkomoko yacyo. Mu bindi bihe, asubiza yifashishije ubumenyi rusange, akabivuga.

## Ese umufasha w'ijwi ashobora kumenya indwara yanjye cyangwa gusimbura muganga?
Oya. Umufasha uha amakuru rusange gusa. Ntamenya indwara, ntanaha imiti, kandi ntasimbura inama za muganga w'umwuga. Niba ukeneye inama z'ubuvuzi bwihariye, fata gahunda y'ubujyanama na muganga, kandi mu bihe by'ihutirwa hamagara nimero y'ihutirwa iri mu gace utuyemo.

## Ese umufasha w'ijwi yibuka ikiganiro cyacu?
Mu kiganiro kimwe, umufasha yibuka ibibazo wamubajije vuba, bityo ukabasha kongera kubaza ibindi bijyanye nabyo. Gukanda New conversation (Ikiganiro gishya) cyangwa guhindura ururimi bisiba iyo bwibuke. Amajwi yandikwa abikwa gusa kugira ngo bunganire iterambere ry'iyi serivisi, mu gihe wemeye ko bikorwa.
