# Ku Bakoresha Ubuvuzi (Healthcare Providers)

## Abaganga n'abaforomo bakoresha MedZen bate?
Abakozi b'ubuvuzi bakoresha MedzenHealth Pro, iboneka kuri pro.medzenhealth.app, kugira ngo bakurikirane abarwayi, bavugane nabo kuri video, banditse ibijyanye n'igenzura, kandi banditse imiti mu buryo bwa elegitoroniki. Abayobozi b'ibitaro cyangwa ivuriro bayikoresha mu gucunga abakozi, amashami, n'uko abarwayi banyura mu bikorwa.

## Ibiri ku dashboard y'abakoresha ubuvuzi ni ibiki?
Dashboard y'abakoresha ubuvuzi yerekana gahunda n'ibiganiro by'uyu munsi, ibisubizo bya laboratwari bikiri gusuzumwa, ubutumwa bw'abarwayi butarasomwa, n'inyandiko z'ubuvuzi zitegereje umukono.

## Abakoresha ubuvuzi bakurikirana abarwayi bate muri MedZen?
Abakoresha ubuvuzi babona urutonde rw'abarwayi bahawe hamwe n'amakuru y'igenzura ryabo riheruka, bafungura umwirondoro w'umurwayi urimo amateka yose y'ubuvuzi, imiti, ibimutera aleji, n'inyandiko za mbere, kandi bashaka abarwayi bakoresheje izina, nomero y'irangamimerere cyangwa nomero ya telefone.

## Inyandiko z'ubuvuzi zikora zite muri MedZen Pro?
MedZen Pro ishobora gukora inyandiko ya SOAP y'agateganyo ikuye mu nyandiko y'ikiganiro cya video, ifashishije ubwenge bw'ikoranabuhanga (AI). Itanga uburyo bwabanjirijwe (templates) ku bijyanye n'ubwoko busanzwe bw'igenzura, ibika inyandiko z'agateganyo mu buryo bwikora, kandi ireka umukoresha wa ubuvuzi ashyiraho umukono w'ikoranabuhanga hamwe n'igihe n'irangamimerere y'uwabikoze.

## Kwandika imiti mu buryo bwa elegitoroniki bikora bite?
Abakoresha ubuvuzi bashakisha imiti mu bubiko bw'amakuru, bagashyiraho ingano, incuro umurwayi ayifata, igihe azayifatira, n'inshuro yongera. Bahabwa integuza ku byerekeye uko imiti itandukanye yavangana, hanyuma bakohereza imiti akoreshejwe kuri farumasi umurwayi yahisemo.

## Abakoresha ubuvuzi basaba ibipimo bya laboratwari bate?
Ibipimo bisanzwe by'ibizamini bya laboratwari bisabwa gukanda rimwe gusa, kandi ibindi bipimo bidasanzwe bishobora guhitamo. Ibisubizo bigera kuri dashboard y'umukoresha ubuvuzi igihe biboneka, kandi ibisubizo bidasanzwe biratahurwa kugira ngo bisuzumwe bwa mbere.

## Abakoresha ubuvuzi bagena gahunda yabo bate?
Abakoresha ubuvuzi bashyiraho amasaha bashobora kubonana n'abarwayi bakoresheje Settings (Igenamiterere), hanyuma Schedule (Gahunda). Bashobora kandi kuzuza igihe cyo kurya, inama cyangwa imirimo y'ubuyobozi. Abarwayi bashobora kwiyandikisha gusa mu bihe umukoresha ubuvuzi aboneka.
