# Ubufasha bwihutirwa

## Ni iki nakora mu bihe by'ubuzima bikomeye bihutirwa?
Niba hari umuntu afite ikibazo gishobora guhitana ubuzima, hamagara nomero y'ubufasha bwihutirwa aho uba, ako kanya. Ntukagire icyizere ko iyi porogaramu izagufasha mu bihe by'ubuzima bikomeye. Ibimenyetso by'iburira birimo kubabara mu gatuza cyangwa kugira ikibazo cyo guhumeka, ibimenyetso by'indwara ya sitoroki nk'uburanga bw'imisatsi y'amaso cyangwa uw'umunwa, intege nke mu kuboko, cyangwa kugira ikibazo mu kuvuga, kuva cyane ku buryo bitanyeganyega, kutakaza ubwenge, igisubizo gikomeye cy'aleji, gukekwaho uburozi, cyangwa kwangirika biteye impungenge bitewe n'impanuka.

## Nabona nte ubufasha bwihutirwa kuri MedZen?
Ku bibazo byihutirwa ariko bidashobora guhitana ubuzima, fungura porogaramu ya MedZen unakande buto yandika "Emergency" (Ubufasha bwihutirwa). Uzahuzwa n'itsinda ry'abaganga bagenzura amakuru, bakazasuzuma imiterere y'ikibazo cyawe bakakwerekeza aho wagenerwa ubufasha bukwiye.

## Uburyo bwa MedZen bwo gusuzuma ikibazo bukora bute?
Mbere na mbere, usobanurira umuforomo wo gusuzuma ibimenyetso byawe. Ikibazo cyawe cyashyirwa mu byiciro bitatu: icyihutirwa cyane, icyihutirwa, cyangwa icyo bisanzwe. Ibibazo byihutirwa cyane byoherezwa mu bwato bw'abarwayi (ambulance) cyangwa mu bitaro, ibibazo byihutirwa bihabwa gahunda y'ubuvuzi mu munsi umwe, naho ibisanzwe bikagenerwa isaha iri hafi iboneka. Itsinda rikwakura amakuru nyuma y'ubuvuzi bwihutirwa cyangwa bwihuse cyane.

## Nagira iki nitegura mbere yo guhamagara mu bihe byihutirwa?
Tegura amakuru ya konti yawe ya MedZen nk'amazina yawe n'itariki y'amavuko, urutonde rw'imiti ukoresha ubu, aleji uzi, n'ibisobanuro by'ibimenyetso byawe, harimo igihe byatangiye, ubukana bwabyo hakurikijwe umubare uva kuri rimwe kugeza icumi, ndetse n'impinduka zishobora kuba zabaye.

## Ni iki gikurikira nyuma y'urugendo rw'ubuvuzi bwihutirwa?
Urugendo rw'ubuvuzi bwihutirwa rwandikwa mu bubiko bw'amakuru y'ubuzima bwawe kuri MedZen, ubuvuzi bwo gukurikirana bugahuzwa binyuze muri porogaramu, kandi ibisubizo by'ibizamini cyangwa amashusho y'imiterere y'umubiri (imaging) byakozwe muri uwo mwanya bigashyirwa mu makuru yawe.

## Nabona nte ivuriro ry'ubuvuzi bwihutirwa riri hafi?
Kanda buto yandika "Emergency" (Ubufasha bwihutirwa) muri porogaramu ya MedZen kugira ngo urebe ibitaro cyangwa ivuriro ry'ubuvuzi bwihutirwa biri hafi yawe. Porogaramu igaragaza igihe usanzwe utegereza mu buryo bwa kirimwe, iyo iryo makuru aboneka.
