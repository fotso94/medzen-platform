# Konti, Igenamiterere n'Umutekano

## Nigute nahindura umwirondoro wanjye kuri MedZen?
Jya kuri Settings, hanyuma kuri Profile, kugira ngo uhindure amakuru yawe bwite, numero ya telefoni n'imeyili. Gumana amakuru yo kuguhamagara ari ay'igihe kugira ngo wakire ubutumwa bwo kwibutsa gahunda z'ubuvuzi n'amakuru y'ibisubizo. Ushobora kandi kongeraho ifoto y'umwirondoro.

## Nigute nakoresha kwemeza mu buryo bubiri (two-factor authentication)?
Jya kuri Settings, hanyuma kuri Security, hanyuma kuri Two-Factor Authentication, ukurikize intambwe zisabwa. MedZen ifasha kwemeza ukoresheje ubutumwa bugufi (SMS) cyangwa porogaramu zo kwemeza (authenticator apps). Kwemeza mu buryo bubiri birasabwa kuri konti zose, cyane cyane iz'abatanga serivisi z'ubuvuzi.

## Nigute nahindura ijambobanga ryanjye?
Jya kuri Settings, hanyuma kuri Security, hanyuma kuri Change Password. Hitamo ijambobanga rifite nibura inyuguti umunani, rivanze n'imibare n'ibimenyetso, kandi ntugasubiremo ijambobanga wakoresheje kuri serivisi indi.

## Nigute nagenzura abashobora kubona amakuru yanjye y'ubuzima?
Jya kuri Settings, hanyuma kuri Privacy, hanyuma kuri Data Sharing. Ushobora gusangira amakuru yawe n'abaguha ubuvuzi bose, abaguha ubuvuzi wihitiyemo gusa, cyangwa ntugasangire n'umwe. Uburenganzira bwo kubona amakuru mu bihe by'ihutirwa buracyahari, kugira ngo abaganga bakwitaho mu buryo bwizewe igihe habaye ibyihutirwa.

## Nigute nakura cyangwa nsiba amakuru yanjye?
Jya kuri Settings, hanyuma kuri Privacy. Export Data iguha kopi yuzuye y'inyandiko zawe, ibisubizo by'ibizamini, imiti wandikiwe n'amateka y'ibyo wasuwe, mu buryo bwa PDF cyangwa JSON. Delete Account isaba gusiba konti yawe; hari inyandiko z'ubuvuzi zishobora kugumishwa mu gihe amategeko abisaba.

## Nigute nahindura uburyo bw'imenyesha (notifications)?
Jya kuri Settings, hanyuma kuri Notifications. Ushobora kugenzura ku buryo bwihariye ubutumwa bwo kwibutsa gahunda z'ubuvuzi, imenyesha ry'ibisubizo by'ibizamini, ubutumwa buturuka ku baguha ubuvuzi, n'ibyo kwibutsa gufata imiti.

## Ni indimi ki MedZen ivugwa?
Porogaramu ya MedZen iboneka mu Cyongereza no mu Gifaransa, kandi ushobora guhindura ururimi ukoresheje Settings, hanyuma Language. Umufasha w'ijwi wa MedZen (voice assistant) yumva kandi agasubiza mu Cyongereza, Igifaransa, Ikinyarwanda, Nigerian Pidgin n'Igiswahili.
