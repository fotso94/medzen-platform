# Ibigo Nderabuzima n'Abaganga

## Nshobora gushaka ikigo nderabuzima kiri hafi yanjye nte?
Fungura porogaramu ya MedZen ujye kuri Care Centers (Ibigo Nderabuzima) mu bipimo biri hasi. Shakisha ukoresheje izina, ubuzobere cyangwa aho biherereye, cyangwa ukoreshe ikarita kugira ngo urebe ibigo biri hafi yawe.

## Nshobora gutoranya ibigo nderabuzima nte hakurikijwe ibipimo?
Ushobora gutoranya hakurikijwe intera, urugero mu birometero bitanu, icumi, makumyabiri na bitanu cyangwa mirongo itanu; hakurikijwe ubuzobere nk'ubuvuzi rusange, indwara z'umutima, indwara z'uruhu cyangwa ubuvuzi bw'abana; hakurikijwe ubwoko bw'ubwishingizi bwemewe; hamwe n'uko bashobora kuguha umunsi umwe ubwiteguro, n'amanota abarwayi babahaye.

## Amakuru ki agaragara ku kigo nderabuzima kimwe?
Buri kigo kigaragaza izina cyacyo, aho giherereye n'uburyo bwo kuvugana nacyo, amasaha gikorera, ibiro n'ubuzobere bitangwa, amanota n'ibitekerezo by'abarwayi, intera iri hagati yawe nacyo, ubwishingizi bwemewe, n'ibihe biboneka byo kubona umuganga.

## Nshobora gufata gahunda ku kigo nderabuzima nte?
Hitamo ikigo nderabuzima, uhitemo ishami cyangwa umuganga, uhitemo igihe kiboneka hanyuma wemeze. Gahunda yawe hanyuma izagaragara kuri My Appointments (Gahunda z'Ubuvuzi Zanjye).

## Nshobora gushaka no guhitamo umuganga nte?
Koresha urutonde rw'abaganga muri porogaramu ya MedZen kugira ngo urebe abaganga, abaforomo n'inzobere hamwe n'amakuru yabo, ubuzobere bwabo, igihe baboneka, n'ibitekerezo byemejwe by'abarwayi. Ushobora kandi kureka MedZen ikaguhuza n'umuganga mukwiye igihe ufashe gahunda.

## Nshobora kubona ubuvuzi ako kanya?
Yego. MedZen itanga serivisi z'ubujyanama bw'ako kanya igihe ukeneye ubuvuzi vuba, hamwe na gahunda zifatirwa mu gihe gikwiye kuri wewe.
