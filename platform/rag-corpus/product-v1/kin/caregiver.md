# MedzenHealth Caregiver

## MedzenHealth Caregiver ni iki?
MedzenHealth Caregiver ni urubuga rwa MedZen rwihariye rugenewe abitaho abarwayi mu rugo. Rufasha abitaho gukurikirana neza ubuvuzi bw'umurwayi, kugenzura ibiganiro n'abaganga (appointments) hamwe n'imiti, no gukurikirana uko ubuzima bw'umuntu bakoreye buhagaze.

## MedzenHealth Caregiver igenewe bande?
Igenewe abagize umuryango cyangwa abitaho b'inzobere bitaho umurwayi mu rugo, kandi bakeneye gukomeza guhuza ibikorwa n'abatanga ubuvuzi ku murwayi binyuze kuri MedZen.
