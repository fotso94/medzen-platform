# Gufata Gahunda z'Isura

## Nafata gahunda y'isura nte muri MedZen?
Fungura porogaramu ya MedZen ujye kuri Appointments (Gahunda z'Isura), maze uhitemo Book New Appointment (Fata Gahunda Nshya). Hitamo uburyo bw'isura, urugero nk'isura aho muhurira, isura ya video, cyangwa isura yo gukurikirana, hitamo umuganga cyangwa uwita ku buzima ushaka, cyangwa uzemerere MedZen agushakire umwe, hitamo umunsi n'igihe biboneka, maze wemeze. Uzahabwa itangazo ryemeza hamwe n'ibyibutswa mbere y'uko ugera ku isura.

## Ni ubuhe bwoko bw'amagahunda nshobora gufata?
Ushobora gufata isura y'ubujyanama rusange, isura ya video, isura yo gukurikirana, isura ku muganga wihariye (specialist), akazi ka laboratoire nk'ifatwa ry'amaraso, cyangwa isura y'ubutabazi bwihutirwa (emergency walk-in). Isura za video akenshi ziba ngufi ugereranyije n'izo muhurira, naho isura ku baganga bihariye zikaba ndende. Igihe nyacyo kirangwa igihe ufata gahunda.

## Nahindura gahunda y'isura nte?
Jya kuri My Appointments (Amagahunda Yanjye) muri porogaramu ya MedZen, hitamo gahunda maze ukande Reschedule (Hindura Gahunda), hanyuma uhitemo igihe gishya. Guhindura gahunda birashoboka mu gihe gito mbere y'isura, kandi porogaramu iragukoresha igihe ntarengwa cy'umuganga wawe.

## Nkuraho gahunda y'isura nte, kandi hari amafaranga atangwa?
Jya kuri My Appointments, hitamo gahunda maze ukande Cancel (Kuraho). Gukuraho gahunda kare, mu buryo busanzwe nibura amasaha 24 mbere y'isura, ntabwo bisaba amafaranga. Gukuraho gahunda bitinze cyangwa kutitabira isura bishobora gutuma umuganga agusaba amafaranga, hakurikijwe amabwiriza yagaragajwe igihe wafataga gahunda.

## Bizagenda bite niba ntitabiriye isura?
Niba witabiriye isura utaraikuyeho, umuganga ashobora kugusaba amafaranga y'uko utaje (no-show fee), hakurikijwe amabwiriza yagaragajwe igihe wafataga gahunda. Ushobora gufata gahunda nshya igihe cyose uva kuri Appointments.

## Ni ryari abaganga baboneka?
Kuboneka bitewe n'umuganga umwe umwe hamwe na Care Center (Ikigo cy'Ubuvuzi), kandi ni ibihe gusa bidafite abandi bisurirwa bigaragazwa igihe ufata gahunda. Abaganga bamwe batanga ibihe by'umugoroba cyangwa iby'icyumweru. Ku bibazo byihutirwa mu gihe kitari icyo abaganga bakorera, koresha buto ya Emergency (Ubutabazi Bwihutirwa) muri porogaramu, cyangwa uhamagare abatabazi bo mu karere kawe.
