# Inyandiko z'Ubuzima n'Ibisubizo by'Ibizamini bya Laboratwari

## Ese nabona bite ibisubizo by'ibizamini bya laboratwari muri MedZen?
Fungura porogaramu ya MedZen, ujye kuri Health Records (Inyandiko z'Ubuzima), hanyuma ujye kuri Lab Results (Ibisubizo by'Ibizamini). Ibisubizo biratondekwa hakurikijwe itariki, aho bishya biba imbere. Kanda ku gisubizo kugira ngo urebe imibare irambuye n'ibipimo bisanzwe bigenderwaho.

## Ese ibimenyetso by'imiterere y'ibisubizo by'ibizamini bisobanura iki?
Pending bisobanura ko urugero rwafashwe kandi rutegereje gusuzumwa. In progress bisobanura ko laboratwari iri kubisuzuma. Completed bisobanura ko ibisubizo byawe biteguye. Normal bisobanura ko imibare yose iri mu rugero rusanzwe. Abnormal bisobanura ko hari umubare umwe cyangwa myinshi iri hanze y'urugero rusanzwe, kandi umuganga wawe azagusanga.

## Ni ibizamini bya laboratwari bikunze gukorwa ni ibihe?
Complete blood count ipima uturemangingo dutukura n'dutukura tw'amaraso, hemoglobine na platelets. Basic metabolic panel isuzuma isukari mu maraso, calcium, electrolytes n'imikorere y'impyiko. Lipid panel ipima kolesteroli na triglycerides. Thyroid panel isuzuma imikorere ya tiroyidi. HbA1c yerekana impuzandengo y'isukari mu maraso mu gihe kingana n'amezi atatu, kandi ikoreshwa mu gukurikirana diyabete. Urinalysis isuzuma ibibazo by'impyiko, umwijima n'imikorere y'umubiri muri rusange.

## Ibisubizo by'ibizamini bya laboratwari bimara igihe kingana gute?
Ibizamini bisanzwe by'amaraso bisanzwe bimara umunsi umwe cyangwa ibiri. Ibizamini bidasanzwe bishobora gumara iminsi mikeya y'akazi, pathology bishobora gumara icyumweru kimwe cyangwa birenga, naho ibizamini bya jenetike bigafata ibyumweru bike. Porogaramu yawe yerekana imiterere ya buri gizamini.

## Nakora iki niba ibisubizo by'ibizamini byanjye byerekana ibidasanzwe?
Umuganga wawe amenyeshwa mu buryo bwikora, kandi ashobora kuguhamagara cyangwa kuguhereza ubutumwa kugusobanurira intambwe zikurikira. Ibisubizo byinshi bidasanzwe ntibikomeye kandi biroroshye gucunga, ku bw'ibyo ntukagire ubwoba. Kurikirana n'umuganga wawe uko yabisabye, kandi ukoreshe porogaramu kugena igihe cy'isuzuma niba bikenewe.

## Nabona hehe amateka yanjye y'ubuzima n'inyandiko z'ibyavuye mu isuzuma?
Jya kuri Health Records muri porogaramu ya MedZen. Uzasanga amateka y'ibyo wasuzumwe, inyandiko z'umuganga, imiti wandikiwe n'ibisubizo by'ibizamini byose ahantu hamwe, kandi ushobora gusaba ko amateka yawe yose yandikwa mu buryo bwuzuye ukoresheje igenamiterere ry'ibanga (privacy settings).
