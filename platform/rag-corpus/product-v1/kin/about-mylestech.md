# Ibijyanye na MylesTech Solutions

## MylesTech Solutions ni iki?
MylesTech Solutions ni isosiyete y'ikoranabuhanga ikora ibisubizo bya digitale ku Afurika n'ahandi, ifite umugambi ugira uti "Guha imbaraga Afurika, igisubizo kimwe cya digitale ku kindi." Imirimo yayo ikubiyemo guteza imbere ubuvuzi hifashishijwe ikoranabuhanga, ikoranabuhanga mu burezi, ikoranabuhanga mu by'imari, ikoranabuhanga mu by'amazu n'ubutaka, ubwikorezi n'ingendo, ubwubatsi bwa cloud na DevOps, ndetse n'ubujyanama ku miyoborere y'impinduka. Igicuruzwa cyayo kizwi cyane ni urubuga rwa MedZen Health rwo kwivuza kure.

## Intego rusange n'icyerekezo bya MylesTech ni ibiki?
Intego rusange ya MylesTech Solutions ni guha imbaraga ubuzima bwiza n'ejo hazaza harimo ubuhanga bushya, binyuze mu kwivuza kure, ubuvumbuzi bwa digitale, n'uburezi bw'ikirenga, bitangirira muri Afurika. Icyerekezo cyayo ni kuba imbaraga nyamukuru za Afurika mu guhindura ibintu binyuze mu ikoranabuhanga, bigatuma ikoranabuhanga rigera kuri buri wese.

## Ni izihe indangagaciro z'ibanze za MylesTech?
MylesTech Solutions ifite indangagaciro esheshatu z'ibanze: ubuvumbuzi mbere ya byose, uburenganzira bwo kugera kuri serivisi kuri bose, kwihaza mu mikorere, ikizere n'ubwisanzure, guha imbaraga binyuze mu bumenyi, no gutekereza ku rwego rw'isi hakurikizwa ibikorwa byo hafi.

## Ni bande bayobora MylesTech Solutions?
MylesTech Solutions iyoborwa na Alain Bagmi, uwashinze sosiyete akaba n'umuyobozi mukuru, Mukum Ngwa, uwashinze sosiyete hamwe na we akaba n'umuyobozi w'ibikorwa, na Mandela Akah, umuyobozi w'ikoranabuhanga.

## MylesTech itanga izihe serivisi?
MylesTech itanga serivisi mu byiciro birindwi: guteza imbere ubuvuzi harimo ibikoresho by'ikoranabuhanga byo kwivuza kure n'amateka y'ubuzima yabikwa muri mudasobwa, ikoranabuhanga mu burezi n'imbuga zo kwiga kuri interineti, ikoranabuhanga mu by'imari n'ubwishyu bwa digitale, ikoranabuhanga mu by'amazu n'ubutaka, porogaramu z'ubwikorezi n'ingendo, ubujyanama mu ikoranabuhanga n'ibikorwa remezo harimo uburyo bwa agile, imikoranire ya interineti, cloud computing na DevOps, ndetse n'ubujyanama ku miyoborere y'impinduka mu guhindura ibintu binyuze mu ikoranabuhanga.

## MylesTech ikora ibindi bicuruzwa ki?
Uretse ibicuruzwa bya MedZen Health, MylesTech ikora Clockwise, urubuga rwiza rufasha mu micungire y'abakozi n'igenamigambi ry'ibikorwa mu masosiyete akomeye. MylesTech na none itanga amasomo y'inshuro ngufi (bootcamp) yigisha ubumenyi mu ikoranabuhanga.
