# Ibijyanye na MedZen Health

## MedZen Health ni iki?
MedZen Health ni urubuga rwa serivisi z'ubuvuzi zikorerwa kure (telemedicine) n'ubuzima bwa dijitali, rwakozwe na MylesTech Solutions. Ruhuza abarwayi n'abaganga, abaforomo n'abandi batanga serivisi z'ubuvuzi, kugira ngo ubone ubuvuzi uri mu rugo, ukoresheje telefoni cyangwa mudasobwa. MedZen yakorewe Afurika, harimo n'imidugudu iri kure, kandi ibice byinshi bikoresha gusa telefoni isanzwe hamwe na SMS cyangwa interineti yoroheje.

## Ni ibiki bigize ibicuruzwa bya MedZen?
Hari ibicuruzwa bitatu bya MedZen. MedzenHealth ni porogaramu y'abarwayi ikoreshwa mu gufata gahunda z'ubuvuzi, guhurira kuri video n'abaganga, kubika inyandiko z'ubuzima no kwandikirana. MedzenHealth Pro iha abakozi b'ubuvuzi ibikoresho byo mu rwego rwo hejuru bijyanye n'ubuvuzi, harimo n'ibikoresho by'ubwenge bw'ubukorikori bifasha mu kwandika inyandiko. MedzenHealth Caregiver ni porogaramu yihariye ku bantu barera abarwayi mu rugo, ikoreshwa mu guhuza ubuvuzi bw'umurwayi no gukurikirana ubuzima bwe.

## MedZen nayikoresha he?
MedZen iraboneka nk'urubuga rwa interineti kuri medzenhealth.app, ndetse n'nk'porogaramu ya telefoni igendanwa ku ba iOS na Android. Abakozi b'ubuvuzi binjira ku rubuga rwabo bwite kuri pro.medzenhealth.app. Uru rubuga rwakorewe abarwayi n'abatanga serivisi z'ubuvuzi bo muri Afurika yose.

## MedZen itanga iki ku barwayi?
Abarwayi bashobora gufata gahunda z'ubuvuzi bagenda ku biro cyangwa bakoresha video, guhurira n'abaganga kuri video ya HD, kwakira urupapuro rw'imiti (prescription) rwoherejwe ako kanya kuri farumasi, kwandikirana neza n'itsinda ribafasha mu buvuzi, kubika inyandiko z'ubuzima bwabo mu buryo bwa dijitali, kureba ibisubizo by'ibizamini bya laboratoire, gucunga imiti n'ibyibutswa, kubona ibigo by'ubuvuzi n'abatanga serivisi biri hafi, gusoma ibitekerezo ku batanga serivisi, no kubona ubufasha bw'ubutabazi vuba biciye kuri porogaramu.

## MedZen Health yakozwe na nde?
MedZen Health yakozwe na MylesTech Solutions, isosiyete y'ikoranabuhanga ifite intego yo guteza imbere ubuzima bwiza n'ejo hazaza h'ubuhanga, hifashishijwe ubuvuzi bukorerwa kure, ibihangano bishya bya dijitali n'uburezi, bitangirira muri Afurika. MylesTech ikora kandi porogaramu z'inyandiko z'ubuzima za dijitali, uburezi, kwishyura, n'imicungire y'abakozi.
