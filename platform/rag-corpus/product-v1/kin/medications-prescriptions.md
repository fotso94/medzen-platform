# Imiti n'Ibyandikwa by'Imiti

## Nabona he ibyandikwa by'imiti yanjye?
Fungura porogaramu ya MedZen ujye kuri Health Records (Amakuru y'Ubuzima), hanyuma ujye kuri Medications (Imiti). Buri cyandikwa cy'umuti kigaragaza izina ry'umuti, ingano ugomba gufata, incuro ugomba kuwufata, muganga wawuwanditse, n'uko byifashe ku birebana no kongera kubona uwo muti (refill).

## Nasaba gute kongera kubona umuti (refill)?
Jya kuri Medications muri porogaramu ya MedZen, uhitemo umuti, hanyuma ukande kuri Request Refill (Saba Kongera Kubona Umuti), maze uhitemo farumasi ukunda. Farumasi itunganya icyo usabye, mu buryo busanzwe mu gihe cy'umunsi umwe, kandi ubimenyeshwa igihe umuti uzaba uteguwe.

## Nashyiraho gute ibyibutsa by'imiti?
Jya kuri Medications, hanyuma ujye kuri Reminders (Ibyibutsa), uhitemo incuro ushaka kwibutswa, urugero, buri munsi, kabiri ku munsi, cyangwa gahunda yihariye. Ibyibutsa bigera kuri wewe binyuze mu butumwa bwihuse (push notifications) kuri telefoni yawe.

## Nakora iki niba icyandikwa cy'umuti cyanjye kirangiye igihe?
Vugana n'umuganga wawe binyuze muri porogaramu ya MedZen usabe ko cyongererwa igihe. Ushobora kohereza ubutumwa bwizewe (secure message) cyangwa gufata igihe cyo gusurwa mu buryo bugufi.

## Nimura gute icyandikwa cy'umuti kikajya kuri farumasi indi?
Koresha uburyo bwa Transfer (Kwimura) buri ku muti muri porogaramu, cyangwa uvugane na farumasi ukoreshaga. Icyandikwa cy'umuti cyawe cya elegitoroniki gishobora koherezwa kuri farumasi nshya.

## Nakora iki ku bijyanye n'ingaruka z'umuti cyangwa uko imiti itandukanye ihura?
Menyesha ingaruka z'umuti binyuze muri porogaramu cyangwa ubibwire umuganga wawe. Ku bibazo bijyanye n'uko imiti yawe ihura (drug interactions), baza umuganga wawe cyangwa umufarumasiye. Mu bihe by'ibyihutirwa, nk'igihe wagize ikibazo gikomeye cy'aleji, hita uhamagara serivisi z'ubutabazi bw'ibyihutirwa zo mu karere kawe.
